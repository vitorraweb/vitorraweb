# Stock photography

Licensed photographs used across the public site where Vitorra has no
photograph of its own good enough for the section. Chosen October 2026.

## Licence

Every image here is from **Unsplash** under the
[Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial use, no
permission or attribution required (credit is given below anyway). None is an
"Unsplash+" image, which is paid and not covered by that licence.

## Rules for using these

- **Never caption a stock photograph as Vitorra's own** fleet, farm, warehouse,
  staff or customers. Captions describe the scene, nothing more.
- **No other organisation's branding as the subject.** Four candidates were
  rejected on exactly this: an ambulance marked "Boston EMS", a medical kit
  carrying its maker's logo and web address, a truck in a supermarket chain's
  livery, and a branded patient monitor. Distant container-line marks on ships
  are unavoidable and acceptable, as for the films (see `/videos/README.md`).
- **Real photographs win.** Where Vitorra has its own image of a thing — the
  head office, the reception, FET fitting work in the field, the SEAL
  manufacturer photograph — that is used instead, and these are replaced as
  real photography arrives.
- All stock images carry the `.q-grade` treatment on the site, so photographs
  from different photographers read as one set.

## Edits

- `kampala-skyline.jpg` is cropped (Unsplash `rect=0,0,6150,4100`) to remove a
  building-sized Johnnie Walker billboard from the right of the frame — an
  alcohol brand cannot be the backdrop of the homepage.
