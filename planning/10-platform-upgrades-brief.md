**For:** Solomon Okello, Chief Executive Officer
**From:** John Oluwaseyi, IT

**In one line:** A set of upgrades that make Vitorra's website and operations
world-class — using free, industry-standard tools, at almost no running cost.

---

## The opportunity

Our platform is already strong. We can now make it *exceptional* — both how
customers experience us and how our team operates — by plugging in proven, free
tools that major companies rely on. Almost everything below has **no licence
fee**; the only small costs are per-message (text / WhatsApp), measured in
shillings, not millions.

---

## What we'll add — by business benefit

**1. Customers hear from us instantly — WhatsApp & SMS**
Order confirmations, payment receipts, and delivery updates sent the way Uganda
actually communicates. Fewer "where's my order?" calls, more trust, a more
professional brand.
*Cost: tools are free; a few shillings per message sent.*

**2. We catch problems before customers do**
The website alerts us the moment anything breaks or goes offline — day or night —
so we fix it before it costs a sale or our reputation. Includes a public
"service status" page that shows customers we're reliable.
*Cost: free.*

**3. Logistics becomes a real digital service**
Customers can see our delivery coverage and track their order on a map, while our
team plans the fastest routes. Turns our logistics arm from a brochure page into
a working product.
*Cost: free.*

**4. Cleaner data, less spam**
Every phone number we collect actually works — essential now that we take
mobile-money payments — and junk / spam enquiries are blocked automatically.
*Cost: free.*

**5. A faster, more premium website**
Visitors instantly find any product or article; international visitors
automatically see dollar prices (and soon their own language). The site feels
quick and high-end — the standard a premium holdings company should set.
*Cost: free.*

**6. We own our audience**
Send our own branded newsletters and offers to subscribers, and answer WhatsApp,
email, and live chat all from one place — instead of renting these services.
*Cost: free tools (a small server if we host them ourselves).*

- **Tiny running costs only:** text / WhatsApp messages (shillings each), and
  optionally a small server (~$5–10 / month) if we host a few tools ourselves.
- **No large upfront investment, and no lock-in** — we can stop or switch anytime.
