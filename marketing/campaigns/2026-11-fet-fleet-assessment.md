# Campaign draft: November 2026 — FET free fleet fuel assessment

**Status:** draft for Marketing to review, then send from Console → Marketing → Campaigns.
**Goal:** booked free fuel assessments (the first step of every FET sale), plus Buyer's Pack requests from people not ready yet.
**Audience:** the FET prospect list in the console (163 organisations), sent as three segments so the opening line fits each reader.
**Attachment:** `vitorra-fet-datasheet.pdf` (already public on the website).
**Links:** every link carries `?utm_source=campaign&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=nov26-fleet-assessment`, so bookings show up as campaign leads in the console.

## Why now (true for each segment)

| Segment | Prospects | The seasonal reason |
|---|---|---|
| Schools | 32 | Third term ends in late November / early December. Buses are parked over the holiday, the easiest time to assess and fit them before Term 1. |
| Public transport + cargo | 36 | December is the busiest travel and delivery month. Every litre saved counts most when vehicles run the most. |
| Manufacturing, distributors, construction, car bonds and others | 95 | Year-end budgets: a cost the fleet already pays every week, set against a one-off, fixed price. |

## What we may say (and what we must not)

- **May say:** in an independent field test by CTI GmbH (Germany, 2025), a VW T5 used 13.9% less fuel with the device fitted. Prices are public, from UGX 1,891,000 with installation included. The assessment is free. There is a 1-year product warranty.
- **Must not say:** that every vehicle saves 13.9%, any savings guarantee, payback promises for a specific fleet, or that the device is approved by any Ugandan authority.
