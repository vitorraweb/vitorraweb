## Market signals

1. **Diesel at Shs 7,290 a litre in Kampala** (30 Oct). Many Kampala pumps quote Shs 7,290 for diesel, and Shs 7,150–7,200 upcountry. The causes are the Shs 200 excise rise from 1 July, a weaker shilling (Shs 3,790 to the dollar in early September, Shs 4,285 on 30 October), and a global shortage of diesel refining. A Makerere economist calls it "the new normal". [Daily Monitor, 30 Oct](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/diesel-hits-shs7-290-rising-fuel-costs-threaten-business-viability-5652850). **FET:** the strongest reason in months for fleets, generator users and construction firms to look at fuel use.
2. **Government says it cannot control pump prices** (7 Oct, Parliament). The Energy Minister said the shilling's fall alone adds about Shs 300 a litre. [Daily Monitor, 8 Oct](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/we-have-no-control-over-fuel-prices-dr-musenero-5623882). **FET:** relief is not coming from Government, so cost control falls on the fleet owner.
3. **Brent fell about 7% on 2 November** on signs of US–Iran diplomatic progress, to roughly USD 104–109. [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/brent-crude-oil), [Forbes, 2 Nov](https://www.forbes.com/advisor/investing/oil-prices-today/). **FET:** Ugandan pump prices lag weeks behind world prices, and the shilling and excise rise stay, so do not suggest prices are about to fall.
4. **School calendar:** PLE papers today and tomorrow (3–4 Nov); Term Three closes **4 December**; holidays to **31 January 2027**. [Daily Monitor](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/we-are-ready-for-ple-says-uneb-5651492), [Pulse Uganda](https://www.pulse.ug/story/education-ministry-third-term-2026-start-date-2026090312354987024). **FET:** idle school buses for eight weeks, the easiest time to assess and fit.
5. **Ebola screening for truck drivers at land borders.** Kenya trained border stakeholders, including truck drivers, at Busia, Malaba and Lwakhakha ([WHO Africa, 9 Sep](https://who-africa.africa-newsroom.com/press/kenya-strengthens-ebola-readiness-at-its-busiest-land-border?lang=en)); a US Embassy Kampala alert on 28 Oct reports mandatory screening of truck drivers at all Ugandan land crossings, and reporting warns it may lengthen transit at Busia and Malaba. Mombasa is also busy with pre-Christmas imports ([Nation, about 21 Oct](https://nation.africa/kenya/counties/mombasa/ebola-festive-season-insecurity-pressure-mombasa-port-5639212)). **Logistics:** importers planning Christmas stock should book now and allow extra days.
6. **Coffee: EU deforestation rules apply from 30 December 2026** for large and medium operators ([EY, 2026](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-0237-eu-deforestation-regulation-application-postponed-to-30-december-2026)). Uganda's September arabica shipments rose 56% year on year while total exports fell 13.9% ([CEO East Africa, citing MAAIF](https://www.ceo.co.ug/uganda-coffee-exports-fall-despite-stronger-september-prices/)). **Coffee:** EU buyers will ask for plot geolocation and due-diligence data; Operations needs our answer before we pitch.

## Claims check

- Do **not** say FET saves 13.9% on every vehicle, or quote a payback for a customer's fleet. It is one test on one VW T5.
- Do **not** say fuel prices will fall because Brent dropped. Uganda's price also depends on the shilling and the July excise rise.
- Do **not** say our freight avoids border delays or Ebola checks. Say we plan for them and keep one named contact on each shipment.
- Do **not** tell EU coffee buyers we are EUDR-ready. That is unconfirmed, and so is our UCDA registration.
- "New normal" is an economist's quote, not our claim. Attribute it if used.
